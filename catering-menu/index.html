FAQ

Catering Questions

Can we customise the menu?

Every menu is fully customisable. The dishes shown are our most-loved options — mix and match across cuisines, add regional specialities, or ask our chefs for something special.

Do you offer pure-vegetarian, Jain or satvik menus?

Yes. We prepare pure-vegetarian, Jain (no onion and garlic) and satvik menus, and can run a separate vegetarian kitchen when required.

Is the food cooked on-site?

For most events our chefs set up a temporary kitchen at the venue so food is cooked fresh and served hot. For smaller events we may cook in our kitchen and deliver in heated containers.

What is your catering price per plate?

Per-plate cost depends on the menu, number of live counters and guest count. Share your requirements and we will send a detailed quote — usually within 24 hours.

Do you provide crockery, tables and serving staff?

Yes. Our catering includes buffet counters, chafing dishes, crockery, cutlery and trained, uniformed serving staff. Premium crockery and butler service are available on request.
