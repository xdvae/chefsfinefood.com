THE CHEF FINE FOOD CATERERS - chefsfinefood.com
================================================

PUBLISH
-------
Upload EVERYTHING inside this folder (not the folder itself) to your hosting:
 - Hostinger / GoDaddy / cPanel: upload to public_html (include the hidden .htaccess file)
 - Netlify: drag this folder onto app.netlify.com/drop, then connect chefsfinefood.com
 - Cloudflare Pages / Vercel / GitHub Pages also work (static site, no build step)
To preview on your computer, just open index.html.

AFTER GOING LIVE (most important for Google)
--------------------------------------------
1. Google Search Console: add chefsfinefood.com and submit https://chefsfinefood.com/sitemap.xml
2. Google Business Profile: create/verify "The Chef Fine Food Caterers" in Jammu and add
   the website link. This is the #1 thing for ranking in "caterers near me" searches.
3. Ask happy clients for Google reviews.

REPLACE BEFORE LAUNCH
---------------------
 - Testimonials: the 4 reviews are SAMPLES. Replace with real client reviews
   (search the HTML for "SAMPLE TESTIMONIALS"), or remove them.
 - Stats (10+ years, 300+ events, 50+ team, 25,000+ guests): confirm they are accurate.
 - Full street address: currently only "Jammu, Jammu & Kashmir". Adding the exact address
   to the footer, contact page and schema helps local SEO.
 - Opening hours: currently Mon-Sun 9 AM - 9 PM.
 - Social links: footer Instagram / Facebook / YouTube links point to "#".
 - About page team cards: generic roles - swap in real names and photos.

PHOTOS & VIDEOS
---------------
Photos are free Unsplash stock images loaded from images.unsplash.com.
To use your own: put photos in assets/images/ and change the img src in the HTML
(also remove the srcset="..." attribute on that image). Keep the alt text descriptive,
e.g. alt="Floral mandap décor at a wedding in Jammu" - this helps Google Images.
Videos: see assets/video/PUT-VIDEOS-HERE.txt

ENQUIRY FORM
------------
No backend needed: the form opens WhatsApp (90551 35000) with the enquiry pre-filled,
or the visitor's email app with "Send by Email".
To ALSO receive enquiries automatically by email, create a free form at formspree.io
and paste its URL into FORM_ENDPOINT at the top of assets/js/main.js.

WHAT'S INCLUDED FOR SEO
-----------------------
 - 15 pages, each with its own title, meta description, H1 and canonical URL
 - A separate page per service targeting "... in Jammu" searches
