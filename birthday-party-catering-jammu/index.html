FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you cater small birthday parties at home in Jammu?

Yes — we cater home and rooftop parties from around 50 guests, with menus and staff scaled to suit.

Can you arrange décor and a DJ for the birthday?

Yes. Theme and balloon décor, cake table styling, DJ with lights and an anchor can all be added.

What food is good for a kids’ birthday party?

Pasta, pizza, momos, spring rolls, honey chilli potato, chaat, burgers and a chocolate fountain are favourites — with a regular menu for adults.

How early should I book?

2–3 weeks is usually enough; book earlier for weekends in wedding season.
