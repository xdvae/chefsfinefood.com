FAQ

Before You Enquire

Where is your office in Jammu?

Our office is at Sector 3, Channi Himmat, near Surya Chowk, Jammu, Jammu & Kashmir 180015. Please call ahead so the right planner is available to meet you.

How quickly will you reply to my enquiry?

We reply to every enquiry within 24 hours — usually much sooner. For urgent bookings, call or WhatsApp us directly on +91 90551 35000.

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. The first consultation, a rough quote and menu suggestions are completely free and with no obligation.

What details should I share?

Your event type, date, approximate guest count, venue or area, and which services you need. Theme ideas, menu preferences and budget help us suggest the best options.

How do I confirm a booking?

Once you are happy with the proposal and menu tasting, a booking advance confirms your date. The balance schedule is shared clearly in writing.
