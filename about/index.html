About Us

Driven by a Passion for People, Food & Celebrations

At The Chef Fine Food Caterers, we believe every celebration is a story — and we are here to make yours extraordinary.

Our Story

A Journey of Food, Hospitality & Beautiful Moments

What started as a passion for great food and warm hospitality has grown into one of Jammu's trusted names in catering and event planning. Over the years we have been part of hundreds of weddings, family celebrations and corporate events — creating experiences that people cherish for a lifetime.

Our journey has always been guided by a simple belief: every event, big or small, deserves exceptional food, thoughtful planning and flawless execution.

Our Journey

How We Grew

01

A Kitchen & a Promise

We began as a small catering kitchen in Jammu with one promise — food cooked fresh, with love, every single time.

02

Weddings Across Jammu

Word spread from family to family, and we began catering full weddings with Kashmiri Wazwan, Dogra specials and live counters.

03

Complete Event Solutions

Families asked us to do more — so we added décor, wedding planning, DJ, bedi setup and valet, all under one roof.

04

Today

A team of 50+ chefs, planners, decorators and hosts serving Jammu, Katra, Samba, Kathua, Udhampur and beyond.
