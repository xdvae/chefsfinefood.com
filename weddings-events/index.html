FAQ

Wedding Planning Questions

Can you manage all the functions of a wedding?

Yes. We plan and manage every function — roka, mehndi, haldi, sangeet, wedding, pheras and reception — with a separate menu, décor and run sheet for each, coordinated by one planner.

Do you arrange the pandit and wedding samagri?

We can coordinate with your family pandit or arrange one, and our bedi team prepares the mandap, havan kund and all samagri according to your customs.

Can you handle destination weddings in Katra or Patnitop?

Yes. We travel with our chefs, décor and service teams for destination weddings in Katra, Patnitop, Udhampur and other locations in J&K. Travel and logistics are included in the quote.

Do you provide separate vegetarian and non-vegetarian kitchens?

Yes. We can run separate vegetarian and non-vegetarian kitchens and serving counters, and offer pure-veg, Jain and satvik menus for families and religious functions.
