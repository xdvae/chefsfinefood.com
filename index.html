FAQ

Questions Families Ask Us

Where are you located and which areas do you serve?

Our office is in Sector 3, Channi Himmat, near Surya Chowk, Jammu 180015. We cater and plan events across Jammu city — including Gandhi Nagar, Trikuta Nagar, Nanak Nagar, Greater Kailash and Bahu Plaza — as well as Katra, Samba, Kathua, Udhampur, Akhnoor and nearby areas. For destination events elsewhere in J&K, call us on +91 90551 35000.

Do you offer pure vegetarian or vegan catering?

Yes. Our sister unit, Shree Shyam Caterers, is part of the same parent company and caters exclusively pure vegetarian and vegan menus — including Jain and satvik food — from a dedicated vegetarian kitchen.

Do you provide catering and décor together?

Yes. We are a complete event company — catering, décor and floral design, wedding planning, DJ and entertainment, bedi and ritual setup, and valet parking can all be booked together with one point of contact, or individually.

How early should we book for a wedding?

For peak wedding season dates (October to February) we recommend booking 3 to 6 months in advance. For smaller functions, 2 to 4 weeks is usually enough — but call us even for last-minute events and we will try our best.

Do you offer food tasting before the event?

Yes. Once your menu is shortlisted we arrange a tasting session with our chefs so you can finalise dishes, spice levels and presentation before the big day.

What is the minimum number of guests you cater for?

We cater for intimate gatherings of around 50 guests right up to grand weddings of 1,500+ guests. Menus and staffing are planned to suit your guest count.
