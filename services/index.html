FAQ

Booking Our Services

Can I book only one service, like catering or DJ?

Absolutely. Every service can be booked on its own. Many families start with catering and add décor, DJ or valet later — we make it easy to combine them.

Do you offer complete wedding packages?

Yes. Our complete packages combine catering, décor, wedding planning, DJ, bedi setup and valet with one coordinator, which saves time and usually costs less than booking separately.

How is pricing worked out?

Pricing depends on guest count, menu, décor scale and the number of functions. After a short consultation we share a clear, itemised quote with no hidden charges.

Do you work at any venue?

Yes — banquet halls, hotels, lawns, farmhouses, community halls and homes across Jammu and nearby areas. We visit the venue beforehand to plan the setup.
