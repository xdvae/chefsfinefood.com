FAQ

DJ & Entertainment – FAQs

Can we give our own song list?

Yes. Share must-play and do-not-play lists, plus tracks for entries and sangeet performances, and our DJ will build the set around them.

Do you provide sound for speeches and rituals?

Yes. We provide wireless microphones and background sound for pheras, speeches and announcements.

Are there sound time limits at venues?

Many venues and local rules restrict loud music late at night. We plan the programme to stay within venue and local guidelines.

Can you arrange dhol for the baraat?

Yes — dhol players for baraat, mehndi and sangeet can be added to any package.
