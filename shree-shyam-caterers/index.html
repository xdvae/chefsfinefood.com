FAQ

Pure Veg Catering Questions

Is Shree Shyam Caterers part of The Chef Fine Food Caterers?

Yes. Shree Shyam Caterers and The Chef Fine Food Caterers are units of the same parent company in Jammu. Shree Shyam is our dedicated pure vegetarian and vegan brand, while The Chef offers multi-cuisine menus along with décor and complete event planning.

Is the food 100% vegetarian?

Yes. Shree Shyam Caterers cooks only vegetarian food in a separate vegetarian kitchen. No meat, fish or egg is ever prepared or served — desserts and bakes are eggless too.

Do you offer vegan or dairy-free catering?

Yes. We create fully vegan menus without milk, paneer, ghee, butter, curd or honey — using plant-based alternatives, cold-pressed oils and dairy-free desserts — and can label every dish on the buffet.

Can you prepare Jain and satvik food?

Yes. We prepare Jain menus without onion, garlic or root vegetables, and satvik menus for pujas, jagrans, kirtans and other religious functions.

Where are you based and which areas do you cater?

We are at Sector 3, Channi Himmat, near Surya Chowk, Jammu 180015, and cater weddings and events across Jammu city as well as Katra, Samba, Kathua, Udhampur and Akhnoor. Call +91 90556 05000 or WhatsApp +91 90551 35000.

Can we combine Shree Shyam catering with décor and wedding planning?

Absolutely. Because both brands are part of the same company, you can pair Shree Shyam pure-veg catering with The Chef's décor, wedding planning, DJ, bedi setup and valet services — with one planner coordinating everything.
